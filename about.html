About the founder

Shantanu Dutta · Prayagraj, India

Shantanu Dutta is building AstraCropz from Prayagraj. The product gives farms and farmer producer organisations a clear crop health score using satellite images — so they can see if a field is getting better or worse without buying expensive tools.

Why AstraCropz

Many farms still depend on walking the field or waiting for visible damage. Satellite data already exists, but it is hard to use if you are not a specialist. AstraCropz turns that data into a simple score, a short report, and a history you can compare over time.

The goal is practical: help FPO managers and progressive farmers check fields faster and share results with members on WhatsApp.

Astrahab

AstraCropz is the first product under Astrahab — a longer effort around space systems that are useful on the ground. Crop monitoring comes first because Indian agriculture can use it today. Comfort-focused space habitats and related ideas remain the wider direction of Astrahab as learning and capability grow.
