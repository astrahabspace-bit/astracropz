How FPOs can use satellite crop checks

Farmer producer organisations often manage many plots. Walking every field every week is hard. A light satellite check helps prioritise visits.

A simple weekly routine

  • Save important plots with names
  • Run checks after major weather events
  • Share the report link on WhatsApp with the plot in-charge
  • Watch for score drops of 10 points or more

Start with a few pilot fields. If the scores match what your team sees on the ground, expand to more members.
