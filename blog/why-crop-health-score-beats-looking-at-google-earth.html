Why a crop health score beats looking at Google Earth

Opening a field on a map app shows you a picture. That picture does not tell you if the crop is stronger than last month, and it does not save a history for your FPO folder.

What a score adds

A crop health score turns the green signal from satellite data into a number between 0 and 100. You can check the same field again later and see if the number went up or down.

  • Easy to share with members
