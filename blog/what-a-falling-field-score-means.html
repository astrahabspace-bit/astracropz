What a falling field score means

If a field score falls by about 10 points or more between checks, treat it as a signal — not a final diagnosis.

What to check on the ground

  • Irrigation gaps or waterlogging
  • Pest or disease patches
  • Nutrient stress after heavy rain
  • Recent harvest or empty patches

Cloud cover and timing can affect a single reading. When in doubt, run another check on a clearer day and compare.
