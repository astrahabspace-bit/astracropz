How this actually helps

Walking every plot every week is hard. Waiting until yellow patches show is late. A field score gives an earlier, wider view so you can decide what to check next.

01

Earlier signal

See if green activity is dropping across the whole field — not only the path you walked today.

02

One number, clear meaning

0–100 score plus plain words: looks good, needs a look, or weak. Easy to share with members.

03

Memory over weeks

Save checks so you know if a plot improved after irrigation or dropped after dry days.

04

Built for FPO scale

Pick a field, get a short report, copy or WhatsApp it. Useful when you manage many members.
